As part of [Good Form](https://goodform.studio/)’s rebrand, our goal was to develop a motion identity that visually expressed the studio’s core strength: stylistic versatility. To highlight this adaptability and appeal to a broader range of clients, the team and I built a dynamic system that showcased [Good Form](https://goodform.studio/)’s ability to move seamlessly across styles and tones.

I was tasked with developing the suite of animations to define the new brand. I created a diverse series of motion pieces, each demonstrating a different aesthetic, yet all unified by a consistent level of quality and creativity. This motion identity became the centerpiece of [Good Form](https://goodform.studio/)'s new website and social media.
