To create the primary on-screen visual for [Deciem](https://deciem.com/) at the prestigious [Tribeca Film Festival](https://www.tribecafilm.com/). The piece needed to be elegant enough for a discerning artistic audience while clearly showcasing [Deciem](https://deciem.com/)'s core products and its unique, lab-centric philosophy.

I used the petri dish—the ultimate symbol of the laboratory—as the central stage for the products. I designed and animated a seamless loop where [Deciem](https://deciem.com/)'s mainline products organically emerged and transitioned into one another. The motion was deliberately crafted to be soothing and hypnotic, drawing the viewer in on a grand scale.
