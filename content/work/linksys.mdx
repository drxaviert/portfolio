I've had the opportunity of designing the animation system for the rebrand of [Linksys](https://www.linksys.com/ca/), with the help of the team at [Bruce Mau Design](https://www.brucemaudesign.com/). Our aim was to create motion that not only reflects the seamless connection between humans and technology but also evokes a sense of harmony and synergy. Each animation element was meticulously crafted to illustrate the fluidity and unity in our relationship with technology.

\*\* For more in-depth reading, please head over the official case study [here](https://www.brucemaudesign.com/work/linksys).
