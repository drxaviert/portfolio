For their new product line launch, [Urban Decay](https://www.urbandecay.com/) needed a bold, high-energy visual campaign that would capture attention on both fast-scrolling social media feeds and high-impact digital billboards.

As part [TUX Creative](https://tux.co/en/) production team, I was responsible for bringing the new products to life in 3D. I modelled and developed a series of style frames and created a short animation that showcased the product’s textures and signature edgy aesthetic.
