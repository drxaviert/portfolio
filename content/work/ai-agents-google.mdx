Google needed a series of 3D motion videos that visualize their AI agents working together for one of their events. They reached out to [Office](https://www.visitoffice.com/), who then brought me on board to help with the project.

Our concept is to create a dynamic array that represents the collaboration between AI agents. While the data itself can appear rigid, the agents work together in an organic, fluid way—merging, adapting, and evolving to accomplish their goal.
