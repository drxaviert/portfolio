[Universal Music](https://www.universalmusic.com/) has reached out to [Good Form](https://www.goodform.studio/) to collaborate on a delightful project for their newly rebranded lullaby channel, '[Lullavibes](https://www.youtube.com/@Lullavibesofficial).'

I was tasked with bringing to life adorable animations of sleeping animals, destined to soothe and captivate the 'tiny audiences'. My goal is to create a visual experience that is both calming and relaxing, perfectly capturing the ambiance of the channel.

\*\* For more in-depth reading, please head over the official case study [here](https://goodform.studio/project/lullavibes/).
