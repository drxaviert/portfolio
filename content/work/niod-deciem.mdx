[Deciem](https://deciem.com/) was launching a new product line centered on advanced bioscience and needed hero visualizations for their commercial video. The core task was to visualize the products' complex ingredients in a way that was compelling, artistic, and true to the science behind them.

[Good Form](https://goodform.studio/) and I was entrusted to create the key scientific elements. We dedicated several weeks to a rigorous R&D phase, studying the ingredients to accurately mimic their physical properties and chemical interactions. We developed a unique visual language that blended scientific realism with an abstract, neon-infused art style to match the product's identity.

\*\* For more in-depth reading, please head over the official case study [here](https://goodform.studio/project/niod/).
