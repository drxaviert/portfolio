For the market launch of [Eroxon](https://eroxon.com/), a new men's enhancer cream, the challenge was to introduce a sensitive product in a way that was clear, trustworthy, and destigmatizing. The campaign needed to build credibility while educating consumers on the product's features.

The team at [Good Form](https://goodform.studio/) and I created the primary advertisement video for the launch. We used a clean, scientific visual style to explain how the product works, maintaining a confident and approachable tone.
