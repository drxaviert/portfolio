Date: 2026-11-17 · Worktree: `audit-break-ui` @ e590d04 · Port 3114 · Scope: work frontmatter → card/carousel, reader (drawer + direct load), nav/tooltips, tracker. All fixtures were temporary local content files + temporary data-only edits (`WORK_ORDER`, `FEATURED_SLUG`, nav/tooltip label arrays, tracker levels); everything is reverted, `git status` is clean.

Method: 30 MDX fixtures (very long / one-word / CJK / Arabic / emoji titles, a 154-char title, long h2, long unbreakable hash + URL in prose and inline code, a decision table with a URL cell, a fenced block with a 200-char line, 32:9 and 1:2 figures, CJK/Arabic prose, emoji, 1,284,932 numbers), plus `WORK_ORDER` at 0 / 1 / 30 items. ## Part 2 — Decisions for you

- **Card title (B3):** clamp to 3 lines and cap the frontmatter title, or let the card grow with the text? Recommendation: clamp + a documented ~90-char limit — the card is a picture with a caption, and 4+ lines already lose the scrim.
- **Fewer than 4 studies (B4/B5):** widen the slots to fit *n*, keep the fixed 4-slot band and accept empty space, or fall back to the mobile stacked layout from `md` up? Recommendation: `repeat(n, 1fr)`, since the band is decorative and the placeholder then has somewhere to sit.
- **Mobile table (B1):** horizontal scroll vs stacked row-per-decision. Recommendation: scroll — it keeps the two-column comparison, which is the point of that table.
- **RTL (B8):** per-field `dir="auto"` vs a `lang`/`dir` per study in frontmatter. Recommendation: `dir="auto"` on text only; the chrome stays LTR.

## Part 3 — What held up

- CJK, Arabic and emoji titles render in card, reader, next-project link and `<title>`; emoji never break the layout; the emoji/grapheme sequence survives at 32px.
- A 154-char title in the **reader** never clips at 375 or 1440 (the page grows instead); the reader title is the only text that handles long values correctly.
- Long hyphenated compounds in an `h2` wrap (balance + hyphens give break points); CJK wraps without line-height clipping.
- 32:9 and 1:2 figures both keep the column width at 375 (aspect only); the 880px breakout works at 1440.
- Fenced code: `white-space: pre-wrap` + `overflow-x: auto` keeps a 200-char line and a sha256 reachable (274px of scroll at 375).
- 30 items: steps by 1 and 3, wrap-around, the `sr-only` "Showing …" announcement and slot geometry stay correct through four rapid steps.
- Drawer via the intercepting route matches the direct load (same title, `data-mode="overlay"`, nav `reader` state, focus on the title).
- Tracker with every cell at level 0 and at level 4 changes colour only — the aspect-square grid cannot reflow; the pager dots are decorative and inside `role="img"`.
- 0 items: `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml` and the OG image still answer 200 (with an empty `urlset`); robots/sitemap derive from `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` (localhost fallback is documented, not a bug).
