# Touch, keyboard & screen-reader audit — 2026-11-17

Read-only audit of `main` at e590d04 (child worktree, no code changes left). Scope: tab order and
focus on `/` and both drawer paths (`/work/[slug]` soft-open and direct load), focus trap and focus
return, Escape, skip link, names/roles/landmarks/headings/lang, tap targets, hover-only affordances,
`touch-action`, iOS-Safari layout, and `prefers-reduced-motion` coverage including WebGL and the
carousel. Method: every relevant TSX/CSS file read, then a production build (`next build` +
`next start -p 3111`) measured with Chromium at 1440×900, 390×844, 375×812 and 375×667, plus
reduced-motion emulation; servers stopped and `.next` + temp logs removed after. Nothing measured on
a real device. **2 High, 3 Medium, 5 Low.**

## Findings

### A1 · High · `src/components/work/expand-shell.tsx:150-188` (`:161`, `:174-188`)
**Problem.** The drawer's focus trap has the wrap points in the wrong order, so forward Tab skips the
whole article and eventually drops focus on `<body>`. **Evidence.** Open `/work/systems-in-motion`
(direct load or soft open), focus lands on the h1; Tab ×8 gives
`Home → Work → Play → About → Design system → Auto → BODY → Home`. The reader's own stops (Work,
Next project, The concept … The outcome) are indices 0-5 in DOM order and the nav is 6-11, but
`nodes = [...stops(dock), ...stops(root)]` makes `last` the article's last tick, so nothing wraps at
`Auto`; the `stranded` rule (`:174-175`) also sends the first forward Tab from the title to `first`
(the nav). Only Shift+Tab from the title reaches the article (walks it in reverse). **Fix.** Keep the
two stop lists separate and wrap per boundary — Tab out of the dock's last stop (`Auto`) focuses the
panel's first stop, Tab out of the panel's last focuses the dock's first — and let the stranded
title continue to the next panel stop instead of `first`. INVISIBLE (pure correctness).

### A2 · High · `src/components/home/home-layout.tsx:160-170`, `carousel/home-carousel.tsx:1770`, `home-cards.tsx:163-176`
**Problem.** Below 768px the carousel is `display:none` and the mobile row keeps one featured card plus
three decorative, `aria-hidden` slivers, so two of the three studies have no link anywhere on `/`.
**Evidence.** At 390 (`hasTouch`) `/`: visible `a[href^="/work/"]` = `["/work/systems-in-motion"]`
only; the `title-sequence` and `type-in-motion` links exist but the `[aria-roledescription=carousel]`
section computes `display: none`; the slivers are `<div aria-hidden="true">`. **Fix.** Give the mobile
row a real way to the other studies (the same carousel at a single-column size, or make the three
slivers real links with labels). VISIBLE (layout/content change) — needs owner approval.

### A3 · Medium · `src/components/nav/nav-dock.css:15-23`, `home/home-layout.tsx:160-196`
**Problem.** On phones the home column is taller than the viewport, so the nav dock — the site's only
navigation and the theme control — starts below the fold at every phone size tested, with no scroll
cue. **Evidence.** 375×667: document 920px, dock top 847 (bottom 911); 375×812: top 847; 390×844:
top 847 vs `innerHeight` 844. The mobile column is tracker 160 + card 420 + three slivers 72/56/56
(+gaps) + nav 64. The 404 page shows the same: dock top 840 at 390×844. **Fix.** Make the mobile
column fit (drop the decorative slivers or shrink the card band) so the dock lands inside `100dvh`,
or pin the dock for the small screens. VISIBLE — needs owner approval.

### A4 · Medium · `src/components/theme/theme-toggle.tsx:1103-1113`
**Problem.** The mobile theme menu opens with focus on a *phase* option ("Clock"), not on the checked
mode, so keyboard/screen-reader users land on an item they did not choose, in a different group.
**Evidence.** At 390 with mode Auto: tap "Choose theme" → `document.activeElement` is
`menuitemradio "Clock"`, while the checked items are `Day:false, Clock:true …, Night:false,
Auto:true`. The effect queries `menuRef.querySelector('[aria-checked="true"]')`, and Clock is first
in DOM order. **Fix.** Scope the initial focus to the mode group (e.g. the checked
`[role=menuitemradio]` outside the `Day phase` group), falling back to the first mode item.
INVISIBLE.

### A5 · Medium · `carousel/home-carousel.tsx:344-357, 1716-1730`, `carousel/carousel-math.ts:45-48`, `home-carousel.tsx:511-526`
**Problem.** Non-front carousel cards are `<a href="/work/…">` that do **not** navigate: activating one
rotates the carousel, and at rest their titles are `opacity: 0`, so a focused side card is a ring
around an unnamed strip. **Evidence.** Focus `[data-slot="1"] a` ("2 of 4: The title sequence"), press
Enter → URL stays `/`, slide order rotates, `[aria-live]` text is empty (keyboard steps are not
announced); the focused card's `[data-car-title]` computes `opacity: 0` and the card is 180px wide.
**Fix.** Render non-front slides as buttons labelled "Show <title>" (or as non-focusable art with
explicit Previous/Next buttons), so the link role only promises a real navigation and the focused
control has a visible, announced name. INVISIBLE-ish (element role/name only; no visual change).

### A6 · Low · `src/app/layout.tsx:60-67`, `work/reader.tsx:32-43`
**Problem.** There is no skip link, and while the drawer is open the page's only landmark is the
`role="dialog"` panel — the article inside it is a plain `div` chain, so landmark navigation offers
just "Primary navigation / Sections" with no way to jump to the study. **Evidence.** No
`href="#…"` skip link in the layout; `Reader` renders `.wd-reader-panel[role=dialog]` →
`.wd-reader-scroll` → `div`, no `main`/`article`. **Fix.** Put the article in `<main>` (or
`<article>`) inside the dialog, and add a skip link to `#wd-reader-scroll` (or `main`) as the first
stop. INVISIBLE.

### A7 · Low · `src/components/home/nav-items.tsx:19, 428`
**Problem.** "Work" is hard-coded `current: true`, so it carries `aria-current="page"` on
`/work/[slug]` too, where the current page is the study. **Evidence.** `LINKS[0] = { label: "Work",
current: true, href: "/" }`; the rendered anchor has `aria-current="page"` in the open drawer.
**Fix.** Compute it from `usePathname()` (`pathname === "/"`). INVISIBLE.

### A8 · Low · `src/components/work/reader.css:140-143, 405-407, 611-613, 631-634, 818-820, 891-895`
**Problem.** Six `:hover` rules in the reader are not gated by `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, so
a tap leaves the link in its hover colour/underline until the next tap (sticky hover) — every other
hover in the app is gated. **Evidence.** `.wd-rail-work:hover`, `.wd-link:hover`,
`.wd-fnref a:hover`, `.wd-backlink:hover`, `.wd-step-replay:hover`,
`.wd-nextlink-link:hover span`; compare `nav-items.css:24`, `ring-hover.css:130`,
`theme-toggle.css:335`. **Fix.** Wrap them in the same media query. INVISIBLE.

### A9 · Low · `src/components/work/reader.css:861-871`, `768-781`
**Problem.** The drawer's "Next project" link is 22px tall (below the 24px floor) and the step-figure
tabs are 30×30, well under the 44px touch guideline with only a 4px gap. **Evidence.** At 390:
`Next project: Systems in motion` measures 358×22; `.wd-step-tab { width: 30px; height: 30px }` with
`gap: 4px`. **Fix.** Give the next-link a padded row (`min-height: 44px`, padding-block) and grow the
tabs' hit area with a `::after` inset (the Replay chip already does this at `:807-816`). VISIBLE only
if the extra height moves the layout; otherwise INVISIBLE.

### A10 · Low · no `src/app/not-found.tsx`
**Problem.** A bad URL renders Next's default 404: no `<main>` landmark, no in-page link back, and at
390 the nav is again at the fold edge. **Evidence.** `/nope` → 404, title "404: This page could not be
found.", no `main a`, dock top 840 at 390×844. **Fix.** Add `app/not-found.tsx` with a `main`, an
`h1` and a link home. VISIBLE.
