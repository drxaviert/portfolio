## Findings

### C1 · High · The focus ring is the decorative accent, so it fails 3:1 in Dawn and Dusk
`globals.css:396,476,547,619` (`--primary`) is consumed as the *only* focus indicator at `theme-toggle.tsx:573,1557,1590,1619`, `home-nav.tsx:41`, `nav-items.tsx:28,35`, `ring-hover.css:213-216`, `reader.css:148,158,557,618,789,823,899`, `scroll-ruler.css:116`.
`--primary` is defined as the phase accent (= `tracker/4`, `docs/design-system/tokens.md:29,40`); a pastel accent cannot carry a 2px ring. Measured ring colour → ratio against the surface it paints on:

| phase | ring | on `#f2f2f2` | on `#ffffff` | on `#e5e5e5` | APCA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dawn | `#d19ef9` | 1.88 | 2.11 | 1.67 | 25.8 |
| Dusk | `#f0b075` | 1.68 | 1.88 | 1.49 | 20.2 |
| Day | `#3687d9` | 3.34 | 3.73 | **2.96** | 49.2 |
| Night | `#fd6850` | — | — | 6.09 (on `#191919`) | 46.3 |

Day's 2.96 is the toggle segments, which draw the ring at `outline-offset: 0` *inside* the `#e5e5e5` track. Screenshots show a barely visible lilac / peach hairline on the focused nav item.
**Fix:** add a per-phase `--ring` used by every focus rule, leaving `--primary` decorative — Day `--palette-blue-800` (4.03 on `#e5e5e5`), Dawn `--palette-violet-700` (3.72), Night `--palette-red-50`, Dusk needs one new primitive ≈ `oklch(0.58 0.13 63)` = `#af6608` (3.53; no existing amber step is dark enough — `#e08030` = 2.28). **VISIBLE** (ring colour changes).

### C2 · Med · 404 / default error pages ignore the phase tokens; Night shows a white page
No `src/app/not-found.tsx` exists, so Next's built-in not-found injects an **unlayered** `body{color:#000;background:#fff}` stylesheet, which outranks the `@layer base` body rule (`globals.css:746-754`). Verified on the production build: `/nope-404` returns `body` = `rgb(255,255,255)` / `rgb(0,0,0)` in **both** Day and Night, while `html[data-phase]` is `night` and the nav is night-themed. That stylesheet's `prefers-color-scheme: dark` branch can also paint a black page while the site is in Day.
**Fix:** add `src/app/not-found.tsx` (+ `error.tsx`) rendering inside the layout with `bg-surface text-on-surface`, so no foreign body rule is emitted. **VISIBLE**.

### C3 · Med · Selected / current state is a fill at 1.1–1.5:1
- Theme toggle capsule `--toggle-selected` on `--toggle-track`: `#ffffff` on `#e5e5e5` = **1.26:1** (Dawn/Day/Dusk, `globals.css:406-407,486-487,557-558`) and `#333333` on `#0d0d0d` = **1.54:1** (Night, `globals.css:629-630`). It is the only state marker (`theme-toggle.tsx:575`); the icon colour barely differs (`#0d0d0d` vs `#4c4c4c`).
- Nav current item `bg-surface-container` `#f2f2f2` on the bar's `#ffffff` = **1.10:1** (`nav-items.tsx:413`, `home-nav.tsx:54`).
WCAG 2.1 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for the visual information that identifies a state. Both are perceptible but faint; the "Work" pill is near-invisible at rest.
**Fix:** give the selected capsule a 1px `--on-surface` edge (19:1) and the current nav item a 1px `--outline` edge, instead of darkening the Figma track to ≈`#949494`. **VISIBLE** → owner decision (the pair comes from Figma).

### C4 · Low · Night secondary text misses the APCA body floor

### C7 · Low · Raw colours outside the token layer

- Drawer scrim literals `rgb(8 8 8 / 0.4)` and Night `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.62)` (`expand-shell.css:32,38`) — a scrim with no token.
**Fix:** point the eyes at a palette token and add a per-phase `--scrim`. **VISIBLE** for the eyes if they should follow the phase.

### C8 · Low · The primitive ramps are not tonal ramps
Converted to oklch (`globals.css:233-322`): hue spread is **Violet 35°** (282–313), **Amber 52°** (46–98), Rose 27°, Blue 24° — all past the 10° visible-drift threshold. Steps also collide in lightness (blue-500 0.757 / blue-550 0.756; violet-350 0.860 / violet-400 0.855; amber-100 0.983 / amber-150 0.982) while the neutral ramp jumps ΔL 0.122 and 0.173 between neighbours. Nothing breaks today because roles pick single steps, but a step number does not predict brightness.
**Fix:** rebuild each family on one H with even L (`building-scales.md`). **INVISIBLE** naming; **VISIBLE** for any step that moves.

### C9 · Low · `theme-color` does not match the painted page top
`layout.tsx:43` uses `#e5e5e5` (`--surface`) for the light scheme, but above the fold the page paints `--horizon-base` `#f1f3f6` (`globals.css:414`, verified top-left pixel of `/`). Night is close (`#0d0d0d` vs `#090909`).
**Fix:** use the horizon base for the light entry. **VISIBLE** in browser chrome only.
